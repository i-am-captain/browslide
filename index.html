Browslide

Presentations in a single HTML file. No server, no build, no account — it runs entirely in your browser, even offline.

Launch Browslide
  • Edit slides with layouts, themes and transitions
  • Present fullscreen with keyboard navigation
  • Save your deck as one portable .html file
  • Export a viewer-only copy for sharing
  • Embed images and video; identical files stored once

Your work never leaves your machine — saving downloads a copy of the file.
